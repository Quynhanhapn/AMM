About AMM

From scientific research to finished products

AMM – Germany Nutrition researches, manufactures and supplies science-based nutrition solutions using modern technology.

From product direction, formula research and ingredient selection to manufacturing and quality control, AMM accompanies partners throughout the product development journey.

Beyond contract manufacturing, AMM aims to be a strategic partner that helps businesses build valuable, long-term products for the market.
