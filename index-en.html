From scientific research to finished products

AMM – Germany Nutrition researches, manufactures and provides science-based nutrition solutions using modern technology.

From product strategy and formulation to ingredient selection, manufacturing and quality control, AMM supports partners throughout the product development journey.

Beyond contract manufacturing, AMM aims to be a strategic partner creating long-term market value.
